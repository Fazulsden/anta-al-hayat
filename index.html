    What this is

    A film told from Fazul's eyes, December 2025 to September 2026. The camera is him; Fabeha is drawn from her photographs: the long hair with the centre part, the sweater and sunglasses from Malam Jabba, the tan coat and beanie from the snow, the IBA sweater and backpack.

    Every line in quotation marks is from the chat, with the spelling kept. The narration between them is written in his voice from what he said at the time. Chat scenes show the real messages with their real timestamps. Some of her real voice notes play inside the film; all of them are kept below. The birthday page is re-created from the real one, letter and game and all.

    Each frame is painted live in the browser and the score is synthesised as it plays. Click the picture to pause, pick a scene to jump to it. Space plays and pauses; the arrow keys skip five seconds, J and L ten; the comma and full stop keys step between scenes; F is full screen. The film remembers where you stopped, so it can be watched in sittings.

    Where it stands

    Production cut 4. Every stretch of the story now has scenes, drafted from the chat with its real lines and times, and they are waiting for Fazul's notes: keep, cut, or change, scene by scene. Still to come: the moments only he can tell (what was said in person, the calls), the second half of the hour, the rest of her voice notes, and a proper ending.
